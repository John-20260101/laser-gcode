Safety Notes (also embedded in G-code header)
  1. Acrylic vapor is flammable - never leave the machine unattended.
  2. Keep a fire extinguisher nearby; dark acrylic engraves hot.
  3. Do a 10x10 mm test patch first to confirm power/speed before the full job.
  4. Air assist ON to reduce flaming and improve edge quality.
  5. Keep the protective film on the acrylic during engraving to prevent
     smoke staining; peel it off after the job.
  6. Run exhaust/ventilation - acrylic fumes are harmful.
  7. This is an ENGRAVE job (power intentionally below cut-through).
     Do not raise power without re-testing to avoid melting/warping.

Usage
  - Home the machine, set work origin at the lower-left of where you want
    the star to appear (the G-code places the star 5 mm in from origin).
  - Focus the laser on the acrylic surface before running.
  - Run with the lid closed and air assist on.
