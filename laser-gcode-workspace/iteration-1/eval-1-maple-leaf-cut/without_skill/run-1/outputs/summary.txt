Maple Leaf Outline — Laser Cut Summary
========================================
File: maple_leaf_cut.gcode

Material : 3mm basswood (椴木板)
Laser   : 10W diode laser
Target  : ~80mm x 80mm (normalized 0-100 grid scaled by 0.8)

Cut Parameters
--------------
Power   : 100% (S1000, full 10W)
Feed    : 250 mm/min (~4.2 mm/s)
Passes  : 2

Coordinate System
-----------------
Origin : TOP-LEFT
Y axis : increases downward (screen/image style)
Scale  : x_mm = x_norm * 0.8, y_mm = y_norm * 0.8

Safety Notes
------------
- ALWAYS verify laser is OFF (M5) before leaving the machine.
- Wear certified laser safety goggles (for your laser's wavelength, ~450nm diode).
- Keep a fire extinguisher nearby; basswood can char/flame.
- Do not leave the machine unattended while cutting.
- Run a small test cut first to confirm focus and through-cut on YOUR material.
- Ensure good ventilation/exhaust; diode cutting produces smoke.
- If your controller uses a different S-max (e.g. S255), rescale the S value.
- If your machine origin is BOTTOM-LEFT (GRBL default), mirror Y:
    y_new = 80 - y_old  (or set $23=1 to invert Y homing).

Tuning Tips
-----------
- If not cutting through: reduce feed to ~150-200 mm/min or add a 3rd pass.
- If excessive charring: increase feed to ~300-350 mm/min or reduce passes to 1.
- Focus is critical: set focus at the material surface for best cut quality.
